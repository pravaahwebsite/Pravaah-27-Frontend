MODULE 02 // RULES

ACCOMMODATION GUIDELINES

01

A day is a 24-hour period from 9:00 AM to 9:00 AM the next day.

02

Accommodation is allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

03

Accommodation pass acts as a visitor pass; Day/Fest pass is required for events.

04

Food is not included; pay separately at any mess.

05

Damage to hostel property is chargeable.

06

Boys and girls have separate hostels; entry into the opposite gender’s room is prohibited.

07

Check-out by 9:00 AM on departure day.

08

Single Room Double Occupancy includes 1 cot, 2 mattresses, and basic amenities.

MODULE 03 // PROTOCOLS

DO’S AND DON’TS

AUTHORIZED ACTIONS — DO’S

  • Carry college ID and registration confirmation.
  • Maintain cleanliness in rooms and surroundings.
  • Respect hostel and campus rules.
  • Use facilities responsibly.

RESTRICTED ACTIONS — DON’TS

  • No loud music or parties in hostel premises.
  • No damage to property or furniture.
  • Alcohol or prohibited substances are strictly banned.
  • Visitors are not allowed without prior permission.
