ACCOMMODATION

Campus residency information and guidelines for Pravaah '26 participants.

⚠️

Important Accommodation Rules

  • ➤ IIT Bhubaneswar students do not need accommodation.
  • ➤ A "day" is defined as a 24-hour period starting at 9:00 AM and ending at 9:00 AM the next day.
  • ➤ Accommodation is allotted on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability.
  • ➤ The pass is equivalent to a visitor pass. To participate in any other events, you must purchase a Day Pass or Fest Pass.
  • ➤ Food is not included in the accommodation. Participants may pay separately and eat at any available mess.
  • ➤ Any damage to hostel property caused by participants will be chargeable.
  • ➤ There are no unisex hostels. Separate hostels are allotted for boys and girls, and entry into the opposite gender's rooms is strictly prohibited.
  • ➤ Participants must check out by 9:00 AM on the day of departure.
  • ➤ A Single Room with Double Occupancy includes one cot, two mattresses, and basic amenities.

IIT Bhubaneswar students do not need accommodation.

Accommodation Guidelines

🛏️

Do's

  • • Carry your college ID and registration confirmation.
  • • Maintain cleanliness in rooms and surroundings.
  • • Respect hostel and campus rules.
  • • Use facilities responsibly.
🚫

Don'ts

  • • No loud music or parties in hostel premises.
  • • No damage to property or furniture.
  • • Alcohol or prohibited substances are strictly banned.
  • • Visitors are not allowed without prior permission.
