PRAVAAH

PRAVAAH, The Annual Cultural and Technopreneurship Fest of IIT Bhubaneswar, celebrates creativity, innovation, and campus spirit. In its 3rd grand edition, it unites culture, technology, and entrepreneurship into one unforgettable experience. “KAIROS — WORLDS UNBOUND” takes participants on a journey through eras and dimensions—where parallel realities converge, reviving the Past with retro-futuristic roots, engaging the Present with entrepreneurial challenges, and exploring the Future through high-tech quantum frontiers and cosmic exploration celebrating the limitless potential of youth and creativity.
