ABOUT PRAVAAH 3rd EDITION

ANNUAL FEST OF IIT BHUBANESWAR

PRAVAAH is the premier Annual Cultural & Technopreneurship Fest of IIT Bhubaneswar. Uniting visionary minds from across the nation, Pravaah fuses engineering challenges, robotics battlegrounds, musical stages, esports leagues, and startup incubation.

FESTIVAL THEME ✦ EDITION 2026 ✦

KAIROS

worlds unbound

Step through the dimensional portal into KAIROS—where parallel realities converge. From ancient retro-futuristic roots to high-tech quantum frontiers and artistic galaxies, enter an unbound continuum of innovation, live performances, and cosmic exploration celebrating the limitless potential of youth and creativity.
