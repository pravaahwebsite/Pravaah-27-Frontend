In Greek, Kairos is the fortunate moment — the instant when everything lines up and you act. Chronos counts the hours. Kairos is the one you remember.

Worlds Unbound is the multiverse behind it. Three genres of Pravaah — technology, culture and enterprise — run as parallel worlds. You can live in one, or cross all three in a single weekend.
